Compatibility

Rift Deck OS runs on most modern x86_64 hardware. Here is what you need.

Minimum Specs

CPUx86_64 dual-core 2GHz
RAM4GB
Storage32GB
GPUVulkan-capable
NetworkWi-Fi or Ethernet

Recommended Specs

CPUQuad-core 3GHz+
RAM8GB+
Storage128GB SSD
GPUGTX 1050 / RX 560 / Iris Xe
NetworkWi-Fi 5 or Ethernet

Confirmed Working

LaptopsThinkPad, Dell XPS, Framework, HP EliteBook
DesktopsCustom PCs, prebuilt towers
HandheldsSteam Deck (experimental), ROG Ally (experimental)

ARM devicesNot supported (yet)
